**The T1-B gate is saturated on this model.**
- FP8 on a single 65 MB layer scores 94.6% top-1, almost the same as all 99 dense layers (94.3%). Stock v0.30's order-dependent BF16 noise scored 93%.
- NVFP4 activation re-quantisation and top-10 routing turn any ulp-level change into ~0.03 KL of token-level churn, independent of the change's size.
- So the 98.5% gate can only be passed by bit-exact levers.
- Task-level quality shows no harm: T1-D, T2 paired, T3 to 64k and V all pass, and NLL Δ is +0.0056 nats overall.

Caveat: in every arm, including the 1-layer one, the reference's top-1 matches the corpus text more often than the candidate's (≈5.0k lost vs 4.3k gained). Parts of the frozen corpus may have been generated by this model, which would bias the scoring toward the reference.
